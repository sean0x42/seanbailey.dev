# Where to browse personal portfolios

Useful when looking at real independent professionals, not just polished portfolio design examples. Availability changes; check the linked profile/site before contacting anyone.

## Hiring-oriented directories

- [Contra Discover](https://contra.com/discover/independents) — Browse independent professionals by skills and other criteria, then contact them from their profile. Contra explicitly presents this as a place to discover contractors and hire talent; clear hiring signal at the directory level, but confirm individual availability.
- [AllDesigners](https://alldesigners.com/) — Independent-designer directory with specialties, activity/popularity sorting, and links out to personal portfolio sites. Useful for exploring a broad range of creative disciplines. Directory is independent-focused, but the pages reviewed do not establish that each person is currently available for hire.

## Inspiration galleries (not hiring directories)
- [PersonalWebsites.org](https://personalwebsites.org/) — Broad directory of thousands of personal sites, including portfolios, blogs, and creative homepages; browse tags such as Developer, Designer, and Web Design. Useful for less-polished and more personal approaches; not a hiring directory.

- [Bestfolios](https://www.bestfolios.com/portfolios) — Curated design portfolio examples, filterable by areas such as UX, graphic, and motion design. Good for visual/case-study inspiration; no clear hire or availability signal in the gallery itself.
- [Awwwards: Portfolio websites](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/portfolio/) — Award-site gallery for visually ambitious portfolio websites. Useful for browsing craft and interaction patterns; an inspiration gallery, not a dependable signal that featured creators are available for work.
- [Arche Directory: Personal portfolios](https://arche.directory/personal-portfolios) — Curated personal-site examples across designers, engineers, art directors, and writers. Useful for broad reference; not a hiring directory and does not signal availability.

## Caveat

A listed portfolio proves discoverability, not current availability. Treat explicit contact/work-with-me language or an active hiring marketplace profile as a starting signal, and verify directly before assuming someone is taking work.
